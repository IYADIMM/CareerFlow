# Founder-operated pilot preparation

Validate one outcome with a small cohort: review a truthful career profile, shortlist permitted opportunities, prepare evidence-supported drafts and agree on next actions. Obtain explicit profile/document consent; define human review responsibilities, role/document limits, turnaround and cancellation terms before taking payment.

Proposed Free, Pro, Executive, Executive Career Accelerator and Coach/Agency prices remain configurable hypotheses. The service-assisted accelerator requires human delivery. This development milestone has no checkout, invoicing or payment-provider integration.

| Record | Meaning | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Prospective revenue | Possible sales or interested customers | Pipeline notes; never cash |
| Invoiced revenue | Charges issued to customers | Issued invoice and terms |
| Collected cash | Payment received and reconciled | Verified provider/bank receipt |
| Operating profit | Recognized revenue less actual operating costs | Accounting reconciliation |
| Safely withdrawable | Cash remaining after obligations and reserves | Founder/accountant review |

Before real users: configure hosted identity and database, test privacy/tenant boundaries, add consent revocation and complete data export/deletion, review terms, obtain authorization for any provider and spending limits, and separate development from customer environments.
