Privacy, terms and release status

Use synthetic information only

This environment is a local development demonstration. Customer use is not approved. This notice describes current behavior; it is not an approved privacy policy or commercial terms of service.

What is stored

Signed-in profiles, pasted CV text, career facts, job descriptions, applications, interview notes and agent records are stored in SQLite on the computer running the API. Session cookies are HttpOnly. Private records are not saved to browser localStorage by the current application. The database is not encrypted by the application.

Processing and sharing

Matching and drafts use deterministic local code. No AI provider, email service, live vacancy retrieval, billing or employer-submission integration is connected. Pasting a CV does not authorize sharing it. Source URLs are references only.

Review, export and removal

Reviewed document versions can be downloaded as authenticated plain text. Settings can clear workspace records. This does not delete account credentials, agent snapshots or audit history. Full customer retention, account deletion, backup expiration and recovery policies require owner approval and implementation before customer use.

Unavailable protections

Hosted identity, email verification, recovery and administrator MFA are not connected. Cross-device persistence and production backups have not been verified. Platform administration and ownership transfer are disabled.

Policies awaiting approval

The platform owner must approve privacy and retention terms, processing disclosures, support contact, acceptable use and commercial terms with appropriate legal advice before a customer pilot. No proposed plan can be purchased here.
